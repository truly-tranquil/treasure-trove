## Warm-up from memory (5 min)

From Week 2, without looking: write a Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 schedule for one subject. Then check [Handout 2-B](../module-2/materials.md#handout-2b).

---

## Core ideas (10 min)

- **You can't manage what you haven't measured.** Most people misjudge their time, usually by overestimating study and underestimating screens, transitions, and "just a minute" detours.
- **The gap is the lesson.** A time audit isn't a grade. The difference between your prediction and your log shows you where your plan and your life disagree.
- **Time leaks.** Small, repeated losses: unplanned phone checks, switching between tasks, a task that took twice as long as you guessed, waiting to start. Look for patterns, not guilt.
- **Track honestly, not perfectly.** Rough 30-minute blocks are enough. A messy real log beats a tidy imagined one.
- **Constraints are not leaks.** If your week is dominated by work, caregiving, or commuting, that isn't wasted time. It's a fixed part of your week, and your plan has to be built around it.

## Learn and set up (15 min)

- **Download and try (15 min):** the Iowa State University Academic Success Center's [Time Audit worksheet (Word file)](https://asc.dso.iastate.edu/files/documents/2026-02/Time%20Audit.docx). Open it, read how it works, and fill in what you've done so far today to see how it feels. No Word? Copy the layout onto paper: days across the top, hours down the side.

---

## Do (10 min now, plus about 5 min a day)

**Set up your audit (10 min).**

1. Choose your tracking method: the Iowa State worksheet, a paper grid, a spreadsheet, or a notes app.
2. Set a phone reminder for the end of each day.
3. Check that your predictions are filled in on [Handout 3-A](materials.md#handout-3a). If you haven't made them yet, do it now, before you log anything else, so the prediction stays honest.

**Track a real week.** Each evening, fill in what you actually did, in blocks of about 30 minutes. Rough is fine. You'll compare your log with your prediction in [Session 4](session-4.md), once you have close to a full week of data. [Session 2](session-2.md) builds your weekly plan from the days you've logged so far, so plan to do it at least two or three days after this session.

**Optional partner step (5 min).** Swap predictions with a buddy before you start tracking, then compare your surprises in Session 4. Share only what you're comfortable sharing.

---

## Options

- **Paper grid, spreadsheet, or notes app.** Use whichever you'll actually open each evening.
- **Voice memo.** Record a short summary of your day instead of filling in a grid.
- **Low-tech floor.** A sheet of paper with a line for each day and a few words per block.
