## Learn (15 min)

## Do (35 min)

**Language audit (10 min).** List your own fixed phrases from the last month ("I'm bad at…", "I'm just not a ___ person," "Some people are just better at this"). Next to each one, jot what it made you avoid, skip, or put off. This is how a belief about ability turns into a choice.

**Reframe relay, solo version (25 min).** Pick two statements from the starter list below, or your own, and complete a [Handout 1-B](materials.md#handout-1b) card for each. Then pick your best reframe and say it aloud once.

**Starter statements:**

1. "I'm just not a writer."
2. "I'll never be good at statistics."
3. "I'm bad at public speaking, so I'll just avoid group presentations."
4. "I failed that test, so I clearly don't belong in this field." (Swap in your own: a midterm, a certification practice exam, a work review.)
5. "I'm just not a numbers person, so I hand off anything with a spreadsheet."
6. Your own (optional).

**Optional partner step (5 min).** Trade cards with a buddy. Each person reads the other's reframe aloud and asks one follow-up question ("What's the first thing you'll try?"). Share only the statements you're comfortable sharing; you never have to show a card.
