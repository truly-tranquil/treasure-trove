# Session 2 — Regulation That Works (≈60 min)

*Optional extras add about 5 minutes: a partner step.*

**Focus:** evidence-based recovery strategies and self-compassion. **CLOs:** 8, 2.

**You'll Make:** two chosen recovery strategies and a rehearsed reframe ([Handout 4-B](materials.md#handout-4b)).

---

## Warm-up from memory (5 min)

Name three of your early warning signs and one sleep hygiene habit you'll try. Then check [Handout 4-A](materials.md#handout-4a).

---

## Core ideas (10 min)

- **Recovery is part of the system.** You can't perform at a steady pace without breaks, movement, sleep, and time with other people.
- **Self-compassion.** Self-compassion means treating yourself the way you would a friend who is struggling: noticing the difficulty, remembering you aren't alone in it, and responding with kindness. Research links it with healthier responses to failure. It is not the same as lowering your standards.
- **A short break works.** A 5-minute self-compassion break uses three moves: this is hard (acknowledge), others struggle too (common humanity), and what would I say to a friend (kindness).
- **Pair a body strategy with a mind strategy.** Examples: a short walk or stretch, slow breathing, time outdoors, music, a call with a friend, a self-compassion break, a cognitive reframe.
- **Reframe under stress.** Reuse your [Week 1 reframe card](../module-1/materials.md#handout-1b) for a stress spike: "I can't handle this" becomes "I can't handle all of this at once yet. What is the first step, and who can help?"
- **Choose what works for you.** Try two strategies and keep what helps. Not every strategy fits everyone.

---

## Learn (15 min)

- **Practice (5 min):** Greater Good in Action, ["Self-Compassion Break"](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/self_compassion_break) (Kristin Neff), a research-tested 5-minute practice.
- **Listen (≈9 min):** Greater Good Science Center, ["Happiness Break: How to Be Your Own Best Friend,"](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/podcasts/item/happiness_break_how_to_be_your_own_best_friend_encore) a guided self-compassion break with Kristin Neff.

You can pass on any practice that doesn't feel right. Pick the one that suits you, or skip to the Do section and use a strategy you already trust.

---

## Do (30 min)

1. **Choose two strategies (5 min).** For example, a self-compassion break and a short movement break. On [Handout 4-B](materials.md#handout-4b), write when you'll use each ("If I notice my first warning sign, then I…").
2. **Practice each once (15 min).** Notice what it did, if anything, and write it down.
3. **Rehearse a stress reframe (10 min).** Pick a real stress spike (an upcoming exam, a bad grade) and run your Handout 1-B card for it. Write the original and growth versions on Handout 4-B.

**Optional partner step (5 min).** Tell a buddy your two strategies and ask them to remind you of them when you're stressed.

---

## Options

- **Written, a voice memo, or a card kept in your planner.**
- **Be realistic.** If a strategy doesn't help the first time, try it again once or swap it.
- **When strategies aren't enough.** If stress, low mood, or anxiety is getting in the way of daily life, reach out to a counselor or doctor. That is a sign of good self-regulation, not weakness. [Session 3](session-3.md) and the [Get Help](../../../get-help.md) page can help you find support.
