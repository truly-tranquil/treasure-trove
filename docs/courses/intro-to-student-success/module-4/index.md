# Module 4 — Well-being, Resources & Sustainability

*"Make It Last"*

**Week 4 of 4** · CLO 8, integrating CLOs 1–7 (course learning objectives, listed in the [syllabus](../syllabus.md))

This week covers sleep, stress, recovery, finding support, and persistence. You finish by putting together your [Personal Student Success Plan](../capstone.md).

[Start Session 1](session-1.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

!!! note "Before you start"
    This week touches on sleep, stress, and mental health. It is educational, not medical or clinical advice. Share only what you want to share, and skip any prompt that doesn't feel right. If you are struggling, Session 3 helps you find support near you, and the [Get Help](../../../get-help.md) page lists crisis lines and ways to find a therapist.

    **If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact a crisis line right away. In the US, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency. Outside the US, contact your local emergency number or crisis service.**

---

## What you'll be able to do

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. Explain the evidence link between sleep, stress, and academic performance.
2. Use at least two evidence-based stress-regulation or recovery strategies.
3. Identify the resources most relevant to your situation (academic, health, financial, social).
4. Explain persistence as something built from several things, weigh the criticisms of the "grit" idea, and plan for likely setbacks.
5. Put together a Personal Student Success Plan that draws on all four weeks.

---

## Your week

About 5 hours of core work: a 10-minute start-of-week check, four sessions (65 + 60 + 60 + 90 minutes), and a 15-minute end-of-week self-check. Session 4 is the longest, because it's where you put your plan together. The sleep and stress log also needs about 2 minutes a day. Optional partner steps add about 20 minutes across the week.

### Start of week · 10 min

- Rate your confidence from 1 (not at all) to 5 (very) on each of the five items above, and write the numbers down. You'll re-rate at the end of the week.
- Write your **average sleep hours** and **stress level (1–5)** for the past week. A rough guess is fine. This is your baseline for [Handout 4-A](materials.md#handout-4a).
- Gather what you made in earlier weeks: Handouts [1-A](../module-1/materials.md#handout-1a) through [1-D](../module-1/materials.md#handout-1d), your [Strategy Toolkit card](../module-2/materials.md#handout-2d) and [spaced schedule](../module-2/materials.md#handout-2b), and your weekly plan, if–then plan, and prep plan from Module 3. You'll use all of them in Session 4. If you've already drafted sections of your plan, bring those too. The [capstone page](../capstone.md) shows which sections you can draft early.

### End-of-week self-check · 15 min

Answer from memory first, then check your notes and the answers.

1. What are three sleep hygiene habits that help?
2. Name two stress-recovery strategies you'll use and when.
3. Who are your first three supports?
4. What are your first three moves after a bad grade?
5. What is one criticism of the grit idea?

??? note "Answers"

    1. Your own. Examples are a steady sleep schedule, a wind-down routine, limiting late caffeine, and keeping screens away before bed.
    2. Your own, tied to a trigger (for example, a self-compassion break when you notice a warning sign).
    3. Your own, from Handout 4-C.
    4. Your own, from Handout 4-C.
    5. Later reviews question how distinct grit is from conscientiousness, and the idea can understate the role of circumstances.

Then re-rate your confidence on this week's five items. If you kept your ratings from the earlier modules, look back over all four and see how they changed.
