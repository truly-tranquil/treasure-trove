# Session 4 — Reading Academic Text (≈60 min)

*Optional extras add about 5 minutes: a partner step.*

**Focus:** structured reading with SQ3R; margin questions; the "say it in your own words" check. **CLOs:** 6.

**You'll Make:** reading log ([Handout 2-C](materials.md#handout-2c)).

---

## Warm-up from memory (5 min)

Without looking, name three parts of a Cornell page and say what each is for. Then check your notes.

---

## Core ideas (10 min)

- **SQ3R.** *Survey* (headings, summary, end questions, 2–3 minutes), then *Question* (turn headings into questions), then *Read* (to answer them), then *Recite* (answer aloud or in writing without looking), then *Review* (go over your notes and questions later).
- **Reciting is retrieval.** Spend more time recalling than reading. If you can't say it in your own words, you haven't got it yet.
- **Adapt it.** For a short or easy reading, survey and recite may be enough. For dense text, work one section at a time.
- **Own-words check.** After each section, explain it in one or two sentences as if to a friend who missed it.

---

## Learn (10 min)

- **Read (10 min):** Stanford Center for Teaching and Learning, ["Reading Efficacy: The SQ3R Method."](https://ctl.stanford.edu/students/reading-efficacy-sq3r-method)

---

## Do (35 min)

**Run SQ3R on a real passage.** Use a reading from a real course, or start at OpenStax *College Success* [section 6.2, "Studying,"](https://openstax.org/books/college-success/pages/6-2-studying) and stop after about five pages. Aim for 3–5 pages so you have time to recite and review, not just read. Write margin questions, recite after each section, and complete [Handout 2-C](materials.md#handout-2c).

**Optional partner step (5 min).** After reading, tell a buddy what the passage said without looking, and let them ask one question.
