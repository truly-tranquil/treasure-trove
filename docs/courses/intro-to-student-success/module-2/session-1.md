## Warm-up from memory (5 min)

From Week 1, without looking: your top challenge and one workaround you already use. Then check [Handout 1-A](../module-1/materials.md#handout-1a).

---

## Learn (50 min)

- **Read (45 min):** Dunlosky et al. (2013), ["Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques,"](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/pspi/1529100612453266/) *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*. This is a long, dense review, so don't read it front to back. Read only:
    - the introduction and overview of the ten techniques
    - the summary of the utility ratings (which techniques rated high, moderate, or low)
    - the sections on practice testing and distributed practice (the two highest-rated)

    Skip the other technique sections. You'll use the ratings in Handout 2-A.
- **Read (5 min):** APS Observer, ["Test Enhanced Learning."](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/observer/test-enhanced-learning-2)

---

## Do (15 min)

**Technique autopsy.** Fill in [Handout 2-A](materials.md#handout-2a) for every technique you used in your last real study session. Use the key under the table to fill in the Dunlosky rating column.

**Optional partner step (5 min).** Compare lists with a buddy and swap one technique each. Share only what you're comfortable sharing.
