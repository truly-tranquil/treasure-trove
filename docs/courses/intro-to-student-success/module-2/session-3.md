# Session 3 — Notes That Work (≈65 min)

**Focus:** active vs. passive note-taking; the Cornell method; the blank-page recall drill. **CLOs:** 4.

**You'll Make:** one page of active notes.

---

## Warm-up from memory (5 min)

Write out a Day 1 / 3 / 7 schedule for one subject from memory, then check [Handout 2-B](materials.md#handout-2b).

---

## Core ideas (10 min)

- **Transcription is not note-taking.** Copying everything word for word keeps your hands busy and your mind idle. Good notes capture ideas in your own words and set you up to test yourself later.
- **The Cornell layout.** Divide a page into a large *notes* area, a narrow *cue* column on the left, and a *summary* strip at the bottom. During the lesson or reading, write notes. Afterward, write questions in the cue column, cover your notes and answer the cues aloud, then write a short summary and review.
- **Active annotation.** For readings, use margin questions, circled terms, and one-line own-words summaries instead of highlighting everything.
- **Blank-page drill.** After any note session, close the notes and write what you remember. This turns notes into retrieval practice.

---

## Learn (30 min)

- **Work through (30 min):** Cornell University Learning Strategies Center, ["The Cornell Note Taking System,"](https://lsc.cornell.edu/how-to-study/taking-notes/cornell-note-taking-system/) including any short videos on the page.

---

## Do (20 min)

**Take one page of Cornell-style notes (15 min).** Use a 10-minute piece of real material: a lecture video, a podcast segment, or a few pages of a reading. Write notes, then add cue questions and a summary.

**Blank-page drill (5 min).** Close the page and write what you remember, then check. Save the page as your capstone deposit.

---

## Options

- **Handwritten or typed.** If you type, use a two-column table for the cue column and notes area.
- **Voice memo.** Summarize each cue question aloud instead of writing the summary strip.
- **No-tech floor.** Fold a sheet of paper to make the cue column and summary strip.
