## Course learning objectives

By the end of STU-101, you will be able to do the following. You'll see these referred to as CLOs (course learning objectives) on the module pages.

1. **Model self:** Describe your own learning profile, strengths, and challenges, and write a personal motivation statement.
2. **Adopt a growth mindset:** Apply growth-mindset thinking to setbacks and reframe fixed "I can't" statements.
3. **Set and govern goals:** Set SMART goals and run a self-regulation cycle (forethought → performance → self-reflection).
4. **Learn how to learn:** Apply evidence-based study strategies: retrieval practice, spaced practice, interleaving, and effective note-taking.
5. **Plan time and work:** Build a realistic weekly plan, break down large tasks (including when procrastination gets in the way), and manage a multi-course workload.
6. **Read and process academic text:** Use structured reading (survey–question–read–recite–review) and annotate actively.
7. **Prepare for assessment:** Use exam and assignment preparation routines that target understanding, not last-minute cramming.
8. **Sustain performance:** Connect well-being, campus and community resources, and persistence to long-term success, and draft a personal success plan.

## Course structure

Each module is one week. It has a start-of-week check, four sessions, and an end-of-week self-check. Each module's overview lists the time for every piece, since the lengths differ from week to week. Plan on about 4½ to 5 hours a week. 

| Module | Theme | Core CLOs | Capstone contribution |
|--------|-------|-----------|-----------------------|
| [1](module-1/index.md) | Foundations & Mindset | 1–3 | "About Me as a Learner" + SMART goals + Zimmerman cycle sheet |
| [2](module-2/index.md) | Learning How to Learn | 4, 6 | Strategy Toolkit card + spaced-practice schedule |
| [3](module-3/index.md) | Time, Procrastination & Systems | 5, 7 | Working weekly plan + chunked big task + exam/assignment prep plan |
| [4](module-4/index.md) | Well-being, Resources & Sustainability | 8 (integrates all) | Personal Student Success Plan (capstone) |

## Capstone

Across the course you build a one- to two-page [Personal Student Success Plan](capstone.md). It covers your motivation, how you learn, how you study, how you run your week, how you stay well, what you'll do if things go wrong, a 30/60/90-day view for the future, and who you'll check in with.

## Support

The course is self-guided, so no one is grading your work or giving feedback. The self-checks at the end of each week are how you find out what stuck. If you have questions or concerns, [get in touch](mailto:tranquil.tyrant@gmail.com).
