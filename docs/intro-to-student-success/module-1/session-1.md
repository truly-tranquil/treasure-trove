# Session 1 — The Learner Inventory (≈60 (90) min)
 
**Focus:** metacognitive self-assessment; identifying your learning profile.
 
**Deposit:** "About Me as a Learner" profile (draft).
 
## Learn (20 (45) min)
 
- **Read (20 min):** OpenStax *College Success*, Chapter 2 ("Knowing Yourself as a Learner"). Start with the chapter introduction, then read section 2.1, "The Power to Learn" (metacognition: thinking about your own thinking).
- **Optional read (25 min):** Section 2.4, "Learning Styles," in the same chapter. It covers what research says about learning styles and how to use your preferences without being boxed in by them.
## Do (40 (45) min)
 
**Four-box inventory (25 min).** Think about the last two courses, training programs, or projects you completed (school or work or independently). What made one easier? What made the other harder? Where did you go when you got stuck? Then fill in four boxes:
 
1. Strengths
2. Challenges
3. Conditions that help or hurt
4. Workarounds I already use
Aim for specific entries, with at least two in Strengths and Challenges and at least one in each of the other two boxes.
 
**Draft your profile (15 min).** Move your best entries onto Handout 1-A (found in Module 1's Materials). Then complete the last two rows: your "why" in one sentence (rough is fine; you'll expand it in Session 3) and one thing you're curious about in how you learn.
 
**Optional partner step (5 min):** only if you want to, trade handouts with a buddy and each name one strength you didn't expect and one workaround you'd never have labeled as such. You choose what to share; skip anything you'd rather keep private.
