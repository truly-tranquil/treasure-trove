# Get Help

If you're struggling, you don't have to figure it out alone. This page collects crisis lines, ways to find a therapist, and free or low-cost wellness tools.

Looking for courses, textbooks, tutoring, and other learning resources? See the [Learning Library](learning-library.md).

!!! warning "In immediate danger?"
    If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US).

As someone who lives in the US, many of these resources are aimed at the US specifically, and international options are listed where I know of them. If you know of a resource I should add, in any country, please email me at [tranquil.tyrant@gmail.com](mailto:tranquil.tyrant@gmail.com).

## 1. Crisis Support

- [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US)](https://988lifeline.org/) — Call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org, any time. Free and confidential, in English and Spanish.
- [Crisis Text Line](https://www.crisistextline.org/) — Text HOME to 741741 (US) to reach a trained crisis counselor by text.
- [The Trevor Project](https://www.thetrevorproject.org/) — Crisis support for LGBTQ+ young people by phone, text, and chat.
- [Trans Lifeline](https://translifeline.org/) — Peer support run by and for trans people.
- [Veterans Crisis Line](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/) — Support for veterans, service members, and their families. Dial 988, then press 1.
- [National Domestic Violence Hotline](https://www.thehotline.org/) — Support and safety planning for people experiencing abuse, by phone, text, and chat.
- [RAINN](https://www.rainn.org/) — The largest US anti-sexual-violence organization, with a confidential support hotline and online chat.
- [National Alliance for Eating Disorders Helpline](https://www.allianceforeatingdisorders.com/) — Support and referrals for eating disorders. (This replaces the former NEDA helpline, which has been disconnected.)
- [SAMHSA National Helpline](https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline) — Free referral service for mental health and substance use treatment.
- [211](https://www.211.org/) — Connects you to local help with housing, food, utilities, and counseling.

### International

- [International Suicide Hotlines (Open Counseling)](https://blog.opencounseling.com/suicide-hotlines/) — Country-by-country list of crisis lines.
- [Find a Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/) — Searchable directory of free, trusted helplines in many countries.
- [Befrienders Worldwide](https://www.befrienders.org/) — Network of emotional support centers around the world.

## 2. Therapy & Counseling

- [Mental Health Coalition Resources](https://www.thementalhealthcoalition.org/resources/) — Curated mental health education and support links.
- [Psychology Today Therapy Finder](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists) — Search therapists by location, specialty, insurance, and cost.
- [Open Path Collective](https://openpathcollective.org/) — Lower-cost therapy sessions for people without insurance or with limited income.
- [FindTreatment.gov](https://findtreatment.gov/) — SAMHSA's locator for mental health and substance use treatment facilities.
- [NAMI (National Alliance on Mental Illness)](https://www.nami.org/) — Education, local support groups, and a helpline for people and families affected by mental illness.
- [Mental Health America](https://www.mhanational.org/) — Free screening tools, articles, and a guide to finding help.
- [Inclusive Therapists](https://www.inclusivetherapists.com/) — Directory centering therapists who serve marginalized and neurodivergent communities.
- [7 Cups](https://www.7cups.com/) — Free peer listening and paid online therapy. Peer listeners are trained volunteers, not clinicians.

## 3. Self-Care & Wellness Tools

- [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/) — Guided meditation, sleep, and stress tools (subscription).
- [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/) — Large library of free guided meditations and talks.
- [Smiling Mind](https://www.smilingmind.com.au/) — Free mindfulness programs for different ages.
- [MindShift CBT](https://www.anxietycanada.com/resources/mindshift-cbt/) — Free app from Anxiety Canada teaching cognitive behavioral skills for anxiety.
- [Greater Good in Action](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/) — Science-based well-being practices from UC Berkeley.
- [Active Minds](https://www.activeminds.org/) — Mental health awareness and peer support aimed at students.
- [The Jed Foundation](https://jedfoundation.org/) — Mental health resources for teens and young adults.
- [Understood](https://www.understood.org/) — Support for learning and thinking differences such as dyslexia and ADHD.
- [CHADD](https://chadd.org/) — Support and education for people with ADHD.
- [Mind (UK)](https://www.mind.org.uk/) — UK mental health charity with clear guides on conditions and treatments. International.
- [Beyond Blue (Australia)](https://www.beyondblue.org.au/) — Mental health information and support. International.
